Hi my name is Samantha Mulholland and I am currently working as a Senior Software Developer at Sciencesoft.com. I started my research in December of 2010 and I am part of the research team within the school of Computing Science. I was born in Scotland and I am currently living in Glasgow.

I enjoy learning new software packages and developing my engineering skills and I am being sponsored by a local Glasgow firm called "Sciencesoft". The company invisage that in order for them to stay ahead of the competition and allow for vastly greater volumes of data to be processed and viewed by their simulator package it will be nessessary to research and develop a solution to meet these demands. My supervisor for my research is Dr Paul Cockshott and my 2nd supervisor is Ron Poet. My academic background mainly consists of web development, animation techniques, 3D modeling and DVD & video production. I have previously studied at Glasgow's Caledonian University and Metropolitan College where I have learned a variety of programming and animation techniques.
